## Cascaded Uncertainty Routing

## Definition

Cascaded Uncertainty Routing is a two-stage detection architecture for tiny object detection on maritime scenes (SOD-TinyPeopleInSea). It routes image regions through a lightweight detector first, then selectively refines uncertain regions with a heavier detector.

## Structure

```
Full image → YOLO (fast full-frame scan) → Uncertainty Router → Faster R-CNN (patch refinement) → Weighted Boxes Fusion → output
```

## Motivation

Most of a maritime image is empty sea — running a heavy Faster R-CNN everywhere is wasteful. YOLO scans the full image cheaply; only uncertain/crowded/tiny regions are forwarded to Faster R-CNN for fine-grained refinement.

## Uncertainty Router — 3 Routing Criteria

1. **Grey-zone score**: `score ∈ [conf_low, conf_high]` — YOLO not confident enough.
2. **Tiny box**: `area < area_thr` — Faster R-CNN's multi-scale RPN handles tiny objects better.
3. **Blind-spot prior**: regions with high prior (horizon, far water) where YOLO may have **zero** detections — critical fallback to prevent cascade blind spot (H4.1).

## Key Design Decisions

- Patches are cropped with **context padding** (1.5×) to give Faster R-CNN surrounding context.
- Overlapping patches are merged before routing to avoid redundant computation.
- Faster R-CNN is trained **only on patches generated by the tuned Router**, not on full images — avoids train/test distribution mismatch.
- Weighted Boxes Fusion merges results from both stages.
