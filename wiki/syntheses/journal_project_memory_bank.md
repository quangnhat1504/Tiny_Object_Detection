The **H-WIoU Journal Project** (Homotopy Wasserstein-IoU for Tiny Object Detection) has been completely decoupled from historical Paper A/Phase 0 exploratory logs into an isolated, self-contained workspace located at [`journal/`](file:///c:/Users/ADMIN/_Project/tiny-object-detection/journal/).
