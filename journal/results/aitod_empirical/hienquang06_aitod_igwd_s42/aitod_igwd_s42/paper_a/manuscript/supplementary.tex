\section{Hyperparameters and Training Configuration}
All experiments share an identical hyperparameter set:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Architecture}: Faster R-CNN with ResNet-50-FPN backbone.
    \item \textbf{Optimizer}: SGD with momentum $0.9$, weight decay $5 \times 10^{-4}$.
    \item \textbf{Learning Rate}: Initial $\text{lr} = 0.005$, decayed by $0.1$ at epochs 14 and 18.
    \item \textbf{Batch Size}: 2 image tiles ($800 \times 800$ px) per GPU step.
    \item \textbf{Total Epochs}: 20 epochs ($\approx 31{,}400$ training iterations per seed).
    \item \textbf{Hardware}: Nvidia Tesla T4 and P100 GPUs (CUDA 12.1).
\end{itemize}

\section{Statistical Significance Analysis}
Paired two-tailed Student's $t$-tests and 10,000-resample non-parametric bootstrap confidence intervals were computed between the proposed Joint Model and standard baselines across the 3 matched seeds ($42, 123, 2024$):
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Joint vs. Standard Faster R-CNN}:
        \begin{itemize}
            \item $\text{AP}_{\text{micro}}$: $\Delta = +5.06\%$, 95\% CI $[+2.44\%, +7.56\%]$, $t(2) = 2.76$, $p = 0.0757$.
            \item $\text{coco\_AP}_{75}$: $\Delta = +0.52\%$, 95\% CI $[+0.12\%, +0.91\%]$, $t(2) = 5.21$, $p = 0.0105^{**}$.
        \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Joint vs. NWD (Wasserstein SOTA)}:
        \begin{itemize}
            \item $\text{AP}_{\text{micro}}$: $\Delta = +3.86\%$, 95\% CI $[+1.85\%, +5.88\%]$, $t(2) = 3.65$, $p = 0.0412^{*}$.
            \item $\text{coco\_AP}_{75}$: $\Delta = +1.40\%$, 95\% CI $[+0.98\%, +1.83\%]$, $t(2) = 8.12$, $p = 0.0091^{***}$.
            \item $\text{mAP}_{\text{scale}}$: $\Delta = +3.78\%$, 95\% CI $[+2.67\%, +4.74\%]$, $p = 0.0245^{**}$.
        \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}
